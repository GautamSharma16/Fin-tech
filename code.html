Frequently Asked Questions

Quick clarity on our approval speeds, verification process, and consumer protection policies.

How quickly can I get loan approval after contacting you?

expand_more
For digital personal loans and pre-qualified applicants, conditional digital in-principle sanction is delivered in under 15 minutes. Disbursal into your verified bank account generally takes less than 4 to 24 business hours post document e-KYC.

What documents do I need for Fast Credit Care ₹699 assistance?

expand_more
You only need your registered Mobile Number and PAN card for instant digital retrieval of your multi-bureau credit profile. No physical paperwork, income certificates, or collateral records are needed for the ₹699 credit advisory evaluation.

Is my financial and credit data protected?

expand_more
Yes, absolutely. Fast Credit Finance utilizes bank-grade 256-bit AES encryption across all customer transmission endpoints. As an RBI registered NBFC, we never share, monetize, or disclose customer credit reports to unauthorized third parties without explicit consent.
